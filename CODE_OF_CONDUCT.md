# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, assume
good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence, and avoid personal attacks,
harassment, discriminatory language, or publishing another person's private information.

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions and restrict participation when
behavior threatens a safe, constructive community. Report conduct concerns privately to
the EnderFall organization maintainers. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far
as practical and apply proportionate, consistent remedies.
